Launch waves

Every region unlocks at midnight on Thursday 19 November in its own time, so the world gets it in waves. Regions that share a moment share a line: Berlin, Paris, Madrid and Stockholm all turn the key at the same second. The UTC column shows that moment on one shared clock, which is why anything east of Greenwich reads Wednesday.
